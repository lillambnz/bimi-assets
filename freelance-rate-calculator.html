🎯 Pricing Strategy Tips

  • Start at Target Rate: Don't undercharge! You calculated your worth scientifically.
  • Offer Package Pricing: Bundle services for higher perceived value (3 blog posts = $X instead of hourly).
  • Raise Rates Regularly: Increase 10-20% every 6-12 months as you gain experience and testimonials.
  • Premium Pricing: Charge premium rates to established clients, complex projects, or rush jobs.
  • Value-Based Pricing: For high-impact work (marketing that generates $10K), charge based on value not time.
  • Never Negotiate Down More Than 10%: Protect your rates. If you discount, add value instead.
  • Test Higher Rates: Every 5th prospect, quote premium rate. You'll be surprised how many say yes!
