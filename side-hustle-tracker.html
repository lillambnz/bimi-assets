📅 30-Day Action Plan

🎯 Week 1: Research & Setup

✅ Choose 2-3 side hustles that match your skills
✅ Research requirements and competition
✅ Set up necessary accounts (Upwork, Fiverr, etc.)
✅ Create portfolio or samples
✅ Set income goal: $______/month

🚀 Week 2: Launch

✅ Create 3-5 service listings/gigs
✅ Apply to 10+ opportunities
✅ Network in relevant communities
✅ Offer intro discount (10-20% off)
✅ Get first client/sale

💪 Week 3: Build Momentum

✅ Deliver exceptional work to first clients
✅ Ask for reviews/testimonials
✅ Apply to 20+ more opportunities
✅ Adjust pricing based on demand
✅ Goal: 3-5 clients/sales

📈 Week 4: Scale

✅ Raise prices (you have reviews now!)
✅ Focus on high-paying clients
✅ Create systems/templates
✅ Reinvest earnings in tools/ads
✅ Goal: $500-1000 earned

💡 Pro Tips for Success

🔥 Start tonight - not tomorrow
🔥 Consistency beats intensity - work 1-2 hours daily
🔥 Underpromise, overdeliver - get those 5-star reviews
🔥 Niche down - be the best at ONE thing first
🔥 Track everything - what gets measured gets improved
