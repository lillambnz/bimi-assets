💡 What is "Passive" Income?

Passive income means you do the work once and earn money repeatedly. Unlike trading time for money (job/freelance), passive income systems earn while you sleep, vacation, or work on other things.

Reality Check: Most passive income requires significant upfront work or money. But once set up, it compounds and scales!

Best Strategy: Start with 1-2 streams, get them profitable, then add more. Multiple passive income streams = true financial freedom!
